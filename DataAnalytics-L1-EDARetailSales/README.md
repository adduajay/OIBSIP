# OASIS INFOBYTE — EDA on Retail Sales Data

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and business opportunities.

The analysis uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to understand revenue trends, customer demographics, product performance, and category-level sales.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Inspect and understand the retail sales dataset.
- Analyze missing values, data types, and dataset structure.
- Calculate mean, median, mode, and standard deviation.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer age groups and gender distribution.
- Identify the top 10 products.
- Analyze revenue by product category.
- Study correlations between numerical variables.
- Identify additional business insights.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Inspection
- Dataset shape
- Column data types
- Missing-value analysis
- Basic statistical summary

### 2. Statistical Analysis
Calculated:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation

### 3. Sales Trend Analysis
Analyzed:
- Monthly sales trends
- Quarterly sales trends

### 4. Customer Analysis
Analyzed:
- Customer age groups
- Gender distribution
- Average sales by customer demographics

### 5. Product Analysis
Identified:
- Top 10 products
- Product/category performance

### 6. Category Analysis
Analyzed revenue generated by each product category.

### 7. Correlation Analysis
A correlation heatmap was created to understand relationships between numerical variables.

### 8. Additional Insight
An additional visualization was created to compare average sales across category and gender combinations.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Total Revenue: **785,876.88**
- Average Transaction Value: **654.90**
- Total Units Sold: **3,501**
- Top Category: **Clothing**
- Top Product: **T-Shirt**

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize inventory and promotional activities for the highest-revenue categories.
2. Use customer age and gender purchase patterns for targeted marketing campaigns.
3. Monitor monthly and quarterly sales peaks to improve inventory and marketing planning.
